🌸 Japan Travel Essential Cheat Sheet

Quick offline reference for emergency & high-frequency travel phrases

🚨 Essential Magic Words

  • Sumimasen: Excuse me / Sorry / Getting attention
  • Arigatou gozaimasu: Thank you very much
  • Kore o kudasai: I want this, please
  • Doko desu ka?: Where is ...?

🍜 Restaurant & Cafe Shortcuts

  • Gonin desu: Party of 5 people
  • Hotto / Aisu: Hot / Cold (Iced)
  • O-kaikei onegai shimasu: Check/Bill please
  • Totte mo oishii desu: Very delicious!

🏪 Konbini & Shopping

  • Fukuro onegai shimasu: Plastic bag please
  • Atatame te kudasai: Please warm up my bento
  • Kādo de / Genkin de: Pay by card / Pay by cash
